Brevo Setup Guide

Follow these 5 steps to connect your free Brevo account and start sending bulk emails instantly.

1
Create a free Brevo account
Brevo gives you 300 free emails per day, forever. No credit card needed.
1Go to brevo.com and click Sign up free
2Enter your name, email and password → click Create account
3Check your inbox and click the verification email from Brevo
4Complete the short onboarding → select Marketing emails as your use case
✓ You're in! You'll land on the Brevo dashboard.
2
Find your SMTP Login
Your SMTP login is NOT your email address — Brevo gives you a special login that looks like a97f21001@smtp-brevo.com
1In Brevo dashboard → click your account name (top right corner)
2Click SMTP & API from the dropdown
3Click the SMTP tab
4Copy the Login value shown — save it somewhere!
✓ Looks like: a97f21001@smtp-brevo.com — copy this exactly.
3
Generate your SMTP Key
Still on the SMTP tab — now generate an API key for sending.
1Click "Generate a new SMTP key" button
2Give it any name like BulkMailer → click Generate
3Click the copy icon next to the key shown
4Paste it in Notepad — you'll need it in Step 5
⚠️ Brevo only shows the key ONCE! Copy it before closing the page. It starts with xsmtpsib-
4
Verify your sender email
This is the email your recipients see as the "From" address — your Gmail, Outlook, etc.
1In Brevo → left sidebar → click Senders, domains, IPs
2Click "Add a sender"
3Enter your name and your email address → click Save
4Open your inbox → click the verification link from Brevo
5Go back to Brevo Senders page — it should now show ● Verified
✓ Your sender email is verified and ready to use.
5
Enter your credentials in Settings
Now paste everything into the Settings page of BulkMailer. Here's what goes where:
Brevo SMTP Login a97f21001@smtp-brevo.com
SMTP Key xsmtpsib-your-key-here
Sender Email your-verified@gmail.com
Sender Name Your Name or Organization
Go to Settings and paste them →
🚀

You're all set!

Once your credentials are saved, go to New Campaign to upload your contacts, write your email, preview it, and send to everyone — all in one place.
